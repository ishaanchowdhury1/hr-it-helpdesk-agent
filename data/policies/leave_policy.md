# Leave Policy (HR-POL-01)

## 1. Leave year
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Annual entitlement
| Leave type   | Days per year | Notes |
|--------------|---------------|-------|
| Casual leave | 12 | Unused casual leave lapses on 31 December. |
| Sick leave   | 10 | Unused sick leave lapses on 31 December. |
| Earned leave | 15 | Up to 10 unused days carry forward to the next year. |

## 3. How to apply
- Apply on the HR portal (hr.goaified-demo.in) at least 3 working days in advance for casual or earned leave.
- Sick leave can be applied on the same day, before 11:00 AM.
- Your manager must approve the request within 2 working days.

## 4. Sick leave rules
- More than 2 consecutive sick days need a medical certificate uploaded on the HR portal.

## 5. Special leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.
- Bereavement leave: 3 days for an immediate family member.

## 6. Leave without pay
If an employee has no leave balance left, extra days off are treated as leave without pay (LWP) and deducted from salary.
