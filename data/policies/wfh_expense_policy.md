# Work From Home and Expense Policy (HR-POL-02)

## 1. Work from home (WFH)
- Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week, with manager approval.
- Fully remote roles are decided by HR in the offer letter.
- WFH employees must be reachable on Slack between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM IST.

## 2. Internet reimbursement
- Employees get up to ₹1,000 per month for home internet.
- Upload the bill on the HR portal by the 5th of the next month.

## 3. Travel and other expenses
- Business travel must be approved by the manager before booking.
- Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days, with receipts.
- Meals during business travel: up to ₹800 per day.
- Approved claims are paid with the next salary.

## 4. Not reimbursed
Personal travel, fines, alcohol and expenses without receipts.

Expense questions: finance-helpdesk@goaified-demo.in
