# IT Policy (IT-POL-01)

## 1. Passwords
- Minimum 12 characters, with at least one number and one symbol.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- After 5 wrong attempts, the account locks for 15 minutes.
- IT will never ask for your password. Never share it with anyone.

## 2. Password reset
- Use the self-service portal (reset.goaified-demo.in), or
- Raise an IT ticket with category "Access". Tickets are answered within 4 working hours.

## 3. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal. Use the Microsoft Authenticator app.

## 4. VPN
- VPN must be switched on when working from public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels).
- VPN problems: raise an IT ticket with category "Network".

## 5. Laptops and equipment
- Every employee gets a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
- Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
- Accessories (mouse, headset, monitor) can be requested by raising an IT ticket with category "Hardware".

## 6. Software
- Do not install unapproved software.
- Request new software with an IT ticket (category "Software"). Approval takes up to 2 working days.

## 7. Contact
IT helpdesk: it-helpdesk@goaified-demo.in
